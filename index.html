Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 3, 2026
Application: PrimeXMR (com.primewellnesstech.liftlog)
Developer: Prime Wellness Technologies
Contact: J.Ayres.Dev@gmail.com

Prime Wellness Technologies built the PrimeXMR app as a freemium health and fitness application. This service is provided at no cost with optional in-app purchases. This page informs users regarding our policies with the collection, use, and disclosure of information.

1. Health and Personal Information (Local Storage Only)

PrimeXMR is designed with an offline-first architecture. All user data—including workout logs, sets, reps, body measurements, sleep hours, recovery check-ins, soreness ratings, profile details (such as age, biological sex, or height), and workout templates—is stored strictly on your device in a local Room database and Android DataStore.

We do not transmit your health, workout, or profile data to any remote developer server or cloud backend. Automatic Android OS cloud backups for this application data are explicitly disabled.

2. Backups and User-Initiated Exports

The app provides features enabling you to export workout data to CSV files or create encrypted full-database backups (protected with AES-256-GCM encryption). In addition, you may choose to generate diagnostic application logs.

  • These files are created locally on your device storage.
  • They are only transferred off-device if you explicitly initiate a share action using the Android system share sheet (e.g., emailing a backup to yourself or uploading it to your personal cloud drive).
  • Separately saved exports and backups remain under your direct control and are not automatically deleted if you uninstall the application.

3. Third-Party Services and Network Data

The application interacts with third-party service providers that process technical and purchase data:

  • Google Play Billing: Handles payment processing for the optional "PrimeXMR Pro" product. All transactions, credit card data, and financial records are handled directly by Google Play under Google's Privacy Policy. We do not receive or store your financial payment details.
  • Google AdMob (Mobile Ads SDK): Used to deliver in-app advertisements. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect device identifiers (such as Android Advertising ID), general IP-derived location, and app performance/interaction data to serve advertisements in compliance with Google’s Advertising Policies.

4. Diagnostic Logs

If you choose to share a diagnostic log to report an issue, the generated log may contain technical runtime details: your device manufacturer and model, Android OS version/API level, system locale, app version, timestamps, and error stack traces. These logs are only accessed if you manually share them with our support email.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Because PrimeXMR does not require user accounts and stores no data on remote developer servers:

  • Your health and fitness data remains on your device until you clear app storage or uninstall the application.
  • Uninstalling the app immediately deletes all local databases and settings managed by the app.
  • As noted in Section 2, external CSV files or backups you chose to export to other storage locations are not removed by app uninstallation and must be managed manually.

6. Children’s Privacy

PrimeXMR is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any updates will be published on this webpage with a revised effective date.
